Ram racing
the official formula sae racing team of colorado state university
We are Ram Racing, an entirely student-run team that designs, builds, tests, and races formula-style race cars to compete against universities across the globe in an ultimate test of our car’s capabilities. Although the heart of Ram Racing is engineering-focused, we value and embrace students from all backgrounds. Every member brings unique skills and perspectives that drive our success. At CSU’s Formula SAE, we don't just learn – we bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world industry challenges. Our graduates stand out, making them among the most accomplished and sought-after talents in the nation.
