QuikTrip plan for Midtown Fort Collins sparks debate
Erin Udell
Fort Collins Coloradoan
March 2, 2026, 5:01 a.m. MT
Despite the public's hopes for more mixed-use residential development, the latest proposal for one of Midtown's busiest intersections comes with a QuikTrip instead.
QuikTrip, which has proposed the construction of a new 6,500-square-foot convenience store and fueling center at the southwestern corner of Drake Road and College Avenue, presented its project development plan to a city hearing officer Feb. 24.
